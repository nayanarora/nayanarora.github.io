About

A standard, carried in one person.

Nayan Arora builds applied intelligence for systems that cannot afford theatre. Civic finance. Climate time series. Spatial operations. Governed AI for Australian institutions.

He is Applied AI Engineer at Sunstone Advisory — a Canberra practice trusted with sensitive government and Defence-adjacent work. Before that: climate production at Exemplary Energy, an ICT business-analyst turn at Xaana.Ai, lead AI engineering at BizFlash, and a technical-manager POC on federated drones at the University of Canberra.

The craft standard was set early. Eleven months as an Apple Specialist in Scottsdale taught what precision feels like in the hand. The rest of the work is that feeling, applied to models, architectures, and rooms where the decision actually matters.

Master of Information Technology and Systems, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, University of Canberra — 7/7, Dean’s List across the degree. Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, cum laude, Arizona State University, with a minor in Engineering Management. New American University Scholar. Teaching assistant and section leader before he ever shipped a climate model.
