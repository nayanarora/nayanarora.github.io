The paradigm

Intelligence that belongs inside the institution.

Not a product. Not a pitch deck. A way of working: take a high-consequence problem, give it a model that can be audited, and leave the organisation stronger than you found it.

Nayan practices applied AI at the point where government, climate, and spatial systems actually run. The work is quiet, sovereign, and human-led. The standard is Apple-grade attention — formed from a year inside the company — brought to Australian civic and defence-adjacent technology.

Climate intelligence

Half-hourly rain from a daily number.

First machine-learning method to disaggregate daily precipitation to half-hourly resolution — with a normalisation layer that conserves the daily total.

Published 2025 in Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment with Harrison Oates, Hong Gic Oh, and Trevor Lee. The model holds across five Australian climate zones. When rolled to hourly, it outperforms prior approaches on most measured metrics.

The same line of work powered climate-data production for 225 weather stations at Exemplary Energy, and a companion paper at the Asia-Pacific Solar Research Conference, Sydney, 2024.
