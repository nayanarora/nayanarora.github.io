2025 · Climate

LSTM sub-hourly precipitation disaggregation

Peer-reviewed in Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment. A two-layer LSTM with a conservation normalisation that forces half-hourly rain to sum to the daily observation. First ML method at this temporal resolution. Validated across five Australian climate zones. Co-authors: Harrison Oates, Hong Gic Oh, Trevor Lee.
